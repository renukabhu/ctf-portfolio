About Me

Hello! I'm Plankton, the mastermind behind some of the most ambitious (and often failed) schemes in Bikini Bottom. Despite my small size, I have a big vision for the future—one where I rule the culinary world and take over the Krusty Krab. I am a genius inventor, an expert strategist, and the proud owner of the Chum Bucket.

My ultimate goal? To uncover the secret recipe of the legendary Krabby Patty and ensure my place as the top restaurateur in Bikini Bottom!
